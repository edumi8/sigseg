\chapter{ISMS Scope}
\label{chap:isms_scope}
Aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 Clause~4.3 guidelines \cite{iso27001}, this chapter
details GreenGrid Energy’s \emph{\ac{ISMS}} scope by identifying the key stakeholders,
describing shared \ac{IT} infrastructure, highlighting core business processes,
and specifying the boundaries within which the \ac{ISMS} will operate.

\section{Shared IT Infrastructure and Systems}
\label{sec:shared_it}

GreenGrid Energy employs a centrally managed \ac{IT} infrastructure used across multiple
departments. Key elements of this shared environment include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Network Infrastructure:} Company-wide network including
          \ac{LAN}, secure Wi-Fi, and \ac{VPN} for remote employees.
    \item \textbf{Server and Cloud Systems:} Hosting internal
          applications, databases, and departmental services.
    \item \textbf{Enterprise Applications:} Microsoft 365, SAP \ac{ERP},
          and \ac{CRM} software supporting business operations and collaboration.
    \item \textbf{User Devices:} Managed desktop computers, laptops, and
          mobile devices.
    \item \textbf{Data Storage and Backup Systems:} Centralized data
          repositories and backup solutions.
    \item \textbf{Communication and Collaboration Tools:} Email systems,
          instant messaging platforms, video conferencing, and intranet portals.
\end{itemize}

Each department relies on these resources for day-to-day operations. Chapter~3 will focus
on specific requirements, objectives, and policies around these systems, while this chapter
captures their basic description for scope purposes.

\subsection{Engineering \& Operations}

The Engineering \& Operations Department serves as the backbone of GreenGrid Energy's infrastructure, ensuring the seamless integration and reliable performance of smart-grid technologies and renewable energy systems. This department is responsible for designing, deploying, and maintaining critical operational assets, balancing efficiency, scalability, and cybersecurity. The \ac{ISMS} scope encompasses key operational processes, addressing risks associated with grid stability, system automation, and industrial control systems while aligning with the organization's broader security objectives.


\subsubsection{Grid Infrastructure Management:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Activities:} Overseeing deployment, maintenance, and modernization of grid components.
    \item \textbf{Data Processed:} Grid topology data, maintenance logs, infrastructure monitoring reports.
    \item \textbf{Cybersecurity Risks:} Unauthorized system modifications, physical and cyber tampering, outdated firmware vulnerabilities.
    \item \textbf{Technologies:} SCADA systems, asset management platforms, industrial-grade firewalls.
\end{itemize}
\subsubsection{SCADA System Operations:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Activities:} Monitoring and controlling grid assets for stability and reliability.
    \item \textbf{Data Processed:} Real-time grid performance data, event logs, control commands.
    \item \textbf{Cybersecurity Risks:} Remote access exploitation, SCADA malware, data interception.
    \item \textbf{Technologies:} Secure remote access solutions, intrusion detection systems, encrypted communication channels.
\end{itemize}
\subsubsection{Incident Response and Disaster Recovery:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Activities:} Developing and executing contingency plans for cyber and operational incidents.
    \item \textbf{Data Processed:} Incident reports, forensic data, response logs.
    \item \textbf{Cybersecurity Risks:} Prolonged downtime, inadequate threat response, lack of forensic integrity.
    \item \textbf{Technologies:} Automated threat detection, backup and recovery solutions, SIEM platforms.
\end{itemize}
\subsubsection{Renewable Energy Integration:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Activities:} Managing the integration of solar, wind, and other renewable energy sources.
    \item \textbf{Data Processed:} Renewable generation data, demand-response metrics, battery storage status.
    \item \textbf{Cybersecurity Risks:} Grid imbalances, data manipulation, unauthorized control access.
    \item \textbf{Technologies:} Smart inverters, edge computing for energy optimization, blockchain for energy transactions.
\end{itemize}


\subsection{Research \& Development (R\&D)}

The \ac{RD} Department plays a strategic role at GreenGrid Energy, providing innovative solutions essential for enhancing smart-grid operations and renewable energy technologies. The \ac{ISMS} scope outlines the department’s core processes, explicitly highlighting the data handled, relevant cybersecurity risks, and utilized technologies, aligning with the \ac{ISMS} objectives.

\subsubsection{Proprietary Research and Innovation Process}

This process involves identifying, researching, and evaluating emerging technologies and concepts that can potentially enhance grid efficiency and reliability.

\textbf{Activities:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Market and technology research
    \item Feasibility analysis and experimentation
    \item Preliminary risk assessment of potential innovations
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Data Processed:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Market intelligence data
    \item Experimental research results
\end{itemize}


\textbf{Cybersecurity Risks:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Data leakage of research findings
    \item Unauthorized access to research environments
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Technologies:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Cloud platforms
    \item Research databases and analytics tools
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Algorithm and Software Development}

This process is dedicated to the systematic creation and validation of predictive analytics algorithms and software for operational use in grid optimization and energy distribution forecasting.

\textbf{Activities:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Specification of requirements
    \item Software coding and iterative development cycles
    \item Simulation-based testing and validation
    \item Continuous security assessments during development
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Data Handled:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Proprietary algorithmic code
    \item Simulated and historical grid data
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Security Risks:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Intellectual property theft
    \item Vulnerabilities in software code
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Technologies:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Advanced simulation tools
    \item Code repositories and CI/CD pipelines
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Prototype Development and Testing}

Prototypes of innovative solutions are built and securely tested to verify their effectiveness, reliability, and resilience to cyber threats.

\textbf{Activities:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Development of technology prototypes
    \item Secure environment configuration for testing
    \item Conducting controlled cybersecurity assessments
    \item Documentation and analysis of testing outcomes
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Data Handled:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Prototype technical specifications
    \item Test results and security assessments
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Security Risks:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Leakage of sensitive design specifications
    \item Unauthorized access during testing phases
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Technologies Used:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Testing and simulation environments
    \item Computing infrastructures
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Intellectual Property Protection}

Ensuring strong protection of intellectual property is essential for maintaining the competitive edge and minimizing risks of industrial espionage and data breaches.

\textbf{Activities:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Documentation and protection of R\&D outputs
    \item Management of patent filing processes
    \item Regular security audits for IP management
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Data Handled:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Intellectual property records and documentation
    \item Sensitive research outcomes
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Security Risks:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Industrial espionage attempts
    \item Intellectual property data breaches
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Technologies Used:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Secure data storage solutions
    \item Encryption and secure communication tools
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Technology Integration and Collaboration}

This process focuses on transitioning validated technologies from the R\&D department into operational use, working closely with Engineering \& Grid Operations.

\textbf{Activities:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Collaborative planning and joint validation sessions
    \item Cross-departmental training and knowledge transfer
    \item Monitoring and support during initial deployment phases
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Data Handled:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Integration plans and documentation
    \item Performance and reliability data from integration tests
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Security Risks:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Integration-related vulnerabilities
    \item Compromise during the operational handover process
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Technologies Used:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Collaboration platforms (Microsoft 365, Jira)
    \item Secure configuration management systems
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Customer Services}
\textbf{Key Processes and Data:} Client contracts, billing data, \ac{CRM}, customer \ac{PII}
\textbf{Security Risks:} Privacy breaches, financial fraud, unauthorized data disclosure

\subsection{Finance}
\textbf{Key Processes and Data:} Financial management (budgeting, transactions, payroll,
\ac{ERP} financial module)
\textbf{Security Risks:} Financial fraud, phishing attacks, unauthorized access, data tampering

\subsection{Supply Chain Management}

\subsubsection{Procurement and Sourcing}
Oversees the end-to-end procurement lifecycle for hardware, software, and services by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Drafting \ac{RFPs} or \ac{RFQs}.
    \item Conducting vendor evaluations and security due diligence.
    \item Negotiating contracts and maintaining supplier agreements.
\end{itemize}
\noindent
Associated data supporting this process:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Vendor profiles and security certifications.
    \item Product specifications (e.g., smart grid components, software licenses).
    \item Pricing details, purchase orders, and invoices.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Vendor Relationship Management}
Manages ongoing relationships with approved suppliers to ensure adherence to GreenGrid Energy’s
security and performance standards by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Monitoring \ac{SLA} requirements.
    \item Tracking vendor performance and compliance metrics.
    \item Conducting periodic vendor risk assessments, especially for critical components.
\end{itemize}
\noindent
Relevant data for this process:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Contract terms and \ac{SLA} metrics.
    \item Vendor incident reports or audit findings.
    \item Communication records (emails, project updates, meeting minutes).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Inventory \& Logistics Coordination}
Coordinates warehousing, transportation logistics, and inventory control once goods or
services are procured by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Tracking shipment and delivery status of hardware components.
    \item Verifying integrity and authenticity of infrastructure parts.
    \item Overseeing storage, handling, and distribution to operational sites.
\end{itemize}
\noindent
Data types maintained in this process:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Shipping documentation (e.g., bills of lading, customs paperwork).
    \item Inventory levels and restocking details.
    \item Asset tracking logs (equipment serial numbers, batch numbers).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Compliance and Risk Management}
Ensures that supplier relationships and procurement activities meet security policies and
relevant regulations by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Reviewing supplier security audits and certifications.
    \item Ensuring compliance with data protection and trade regulations.
    \item Coordinating corrective actions for supplier or logistics risks.
\end{itemize}
\noindent
Data associated with this process:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Audit reports, compliance checklists, and regulatory filings.
    \item Documentation of risk treatment actions (vendor replacements, contract updates).
    \item Internal approval records and compliance sign-offs.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Contract and \ac{SLA} Oversight}
Manages contractual obligations from initial sign-off to renewal or termination by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Monitoring contract milestones (renewal dates, penalty clauses).
    \item Ensuring suppliers meet service quality and security requirements.
    \item Handling contract amendments for security or operational changes.
\end{itemize}
\noindent
Key data involved:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \ac{MSAs}, \ac{SOWs}.
    \item Change requests and contract amendment records.
    \item Legal correspondence regarding contract disputes or terminations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Human Resources}
\textbf{Key Processes and Data:} Employee management (personal records, payroll data,
performance data)
\textbf{Security Risks:} Unauthorized disclosure, insider threats, privacy breaches

\subsection{IT Department}
\textbf{Key Processes and Data:} Infrastructure management (network devices, servers, endpoint devices, IAM systems, incident response data)
\textbf{Security Risks:} System compromise, configuration errors, misuse of admin privileges

\subsection{Sales and Marketing}
\textbf{Key Processes and Data:} Customer relationship management (\ac{CRM}), market data,
public-facing web resources
\textbf{Security Risks:} Unauthorized access, reputation damage, phishing/malware attacks

\section{ISMS Boundaries and Exclusions}
\label{sec:boundaries}

The \ac{ISMS} boundaries include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Internal \ac{IT} and \ac{OT} networks, including \ac{SCADA} and
          enterprise systems used by the Supply Chain Management, Customer Services
          \& Billing, and Research \& Development departments.
    \item Data processing within cloud environments
          generated or consumed by Supply Chain Management, Customer Services
          \& Billing, and Research \& Development.
    \item Procurement processes and vendor management.
    \item Employee data, access management, and training processes.
\end{itemize}

Explicit exclusions from the \ac{ISMS} scope include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Third-party systems fully managed externally, where GreenGrid has
          no direct control.
    \item Personal employee devices not centrally managed by the organization.
    \item Physical security at externally managed premises (e.g., leased data centers).
    \item Separate subsidiaries or partner organizations outside direct oversight.
\end{itemize}

These exclusions are justified by the lack of direct administrative oversight or
practical manageability; any associated risks are mitigated through contractual
obligations and vendor assessments \cite{advisera}.
