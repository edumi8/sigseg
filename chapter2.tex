\section{Scope of the \ac{ISMS}}

Aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 Clause 4.3 guidelines \cite{iso27001}, this chapter outlines the scope of GreenGrid Energy’s \ac{ISMS}, identifying stakeholders, \ac{IT} infrastructure, key business processes, associated data, and explicit \ac{ISMS} boundaries.

\subsection{Organizational Stakeholders}

GreenGrid Energy's stakeholders, as defined by ISO/IEC 27001, include both internal and external entities that influence or are impacted by the organization's security practices \cite{hightable}.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Stakeholders and \ac{ISMS} Interaction}
\label{tab:stakeholders}
\begin{tabular}{|p{5cm}|p{9cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Stakeholder Group} & \textbf{Interaction with the \ac{ISMS}} \\ \hline
Executive Management and Board & Provide strategic oversight, allocate resources, approve \ac{ISMS} scope, and review security performance. \\ \hline
\ac{IT} Department & Implement and maintain technical security controls; daily management of the \ac{ISMS} infrastructure. \\ \hline
Department Heads and Employees & Comply with \ac{ISMS} policies, handle information securely, report incidents, and participate in risk assessments. \\ \hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Shared \ac{IT} Infrastructure and Systems}

GreenGrid Energy employs a centrally managed \ac{IT} infrastructure shared by multiple departments. This infrastructure includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Network Infrastructure}: Company-wide network including \ac{LAN}, secure Wi-Fi, and \ac{VPN} for remote employees.
    \item \textbf{Server and Cloud Systems (AWS/Azure)}: Hosting internal applications, databases, and departmental services.
    \item \textbf{Enterprise Applications}: Microsoft 365, SAP \ac{ERP}, and \ac{CRM} software supporting business operations and collaboration.
    \item \textbf{User Devices}: Managed desktop computers, laptops, and mobile devices.
    \item \textbf{Data Storage and Backup Systems}: Centralized data repositories and backup solutions.
    \item \textbf{Communication and Collaboration Tools}: Email systems, instant messaging platforms, video conferencing, and intranet portals.
\end{itemize}

Specific security policies and controls regarding these technologies are detailed in subsequent chapters.

\subsection{\ac{ISMS} Boundaries and Exclusions}

The \ac{ISMS} boundaries include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Internal \ac{IT} and \ac{OT} networks, including \ac{SCADA} and enterprise systems used by the Supply Chain Management, Customer Services \& Billing, and Research \& Development departments.
    \item Data processing within cloud environments (e.g., AWS or Azure) that is generated or consumed by the Supply Chain Management, Customer Services \& Billing, and Research \& Development departments.
    \item Procurement processes and vendor management.
    \item Employee data, access management, and training processes.
\end{itemize}

Explicit exclusions from the \ac{ISMS} scope include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Third-party systems fully managed externally, where GreenGrid has no direct control.
    \item Personal employee devices not centrally managed by the organization.
    \item Physical security at externally managed premises (e.g., leased data centers).
    \item Separate subsidiaries or partner organizations.
\end{itemize}

These exclusions are justified by the lack of direct administrative oversight or practical manageability; any associated risks are mitigated through contractual obligations and vendor assessments \cite{advisera}.
