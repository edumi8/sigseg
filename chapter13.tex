\chapter{Management Review Simulation}
\label{chap:management_review}

In this chapter, there is a top-level management review of GreenGrid Energy’s Information Security Management System (ISMS), as required by ISO/IEC 27001 Clause 9.3. The goal is to assess the ISMS's suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness in light of strategic objectives, audit findings, and changing risk factors.

\section{Review Objectives}
The objectives of the management review include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Ensuring continued alignment between the ISMS and business strategy.
    \item Evaluating ISMS performance indicators, audit results, and risk status.
    \item Identifying changes in external or internal issues relevant to information security.
    \item Confirming the adequacy of available resources for maintaining and improving the ISMS.
    \item Assessing opportunities for continual improvement.
\end{itemize}

\section{Review Inputs}
The following inputs were considered in the management review meeting:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Internal Audit Results} (Activity 13): Summary of audit findings and corrective actions required.
    \item \textbf{Risk Assessment Update} (Activity 12): Residual risk levels following control application.
    \item \textbf{ISMS Objectives Progress} (Activity 3.6): Measurement of key departmental objectives (e.g., EO-1, RD-1, SC-1).
    \item \textbf{Non-Conformities and Corrective Actions}: Three non-conformities were identified and are being addressed.
    \item \textbf{Changes in Threat Landscape}: Growing risk of nation-state activity and AI-powered malware targeting smart infrastructure.
    \item \textbf{Feedback from Departments}: Requests for improved incident response coordination and better forensic logging.
\end{itemize}

\section{Review Outcomes}
Based on the discussion, the management team concluded the following:

\subsection{ISMS Effectiveness}
The ISMS was deemed effective in:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Mitigating high-risk threats to OT and IT infrastructure.
    \item Supporting departmental security objectives with measurable progress.
    \item Aligning security practices with ISO/IEC 27001 and NIS2 compliance requirements.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Improvements Identified}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Establishing a \textbf{centralised incident response dashboard} to unify alerts from OT and IT systems.
    \item Enhancing \textbf{VPN and SCADA change management} through stricter access logs and certificate rotation.
    \item Incorporating real-time audit log analysis into R\&D systems using existing SIEM infrastructure.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Resource Allocation}
Management approved:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Additional budget for upgrading VPN infrastructure.
    \item Dedicated security engineer support for the R\&D and Engineering departments.
    \item Training sessions for incident handling and forensic readiness.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future Review Plan}
The next ISMS review is scheduled for Q1 2026, with a focus on:
\begin{itemize}
    \item External certification readiness.
    \item Maturity benchmarking against other industry players.
    \item Review of ongoing incident metrics and updated regulatory requirements.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
The management review demonstrated a high degree of commitment to the continual improvement of GreenGrid Energy’s ISMS. Identified issues are being addressed through corrective actions, and new improvement opportunities were endorsed by leadership. The ISMS is considered mature and well-prepared for external audit and certification.
