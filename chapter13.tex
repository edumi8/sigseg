\chapter{Management Review Simulation}
\label{chap:management_review}

This chapter simulate the top-management
review required by ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 9.3 and by providing a
critical reflection on the work performed throughout the \ac{ISMS} project.
It is structured as two self-contained deliverables:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Management Review Report} — documentation of the simulated
        review meeting, including required inputs, decisions, and actions
        (Clause 9.3.2–9.3.3);
  \item \textbf{Critical Reflection Report} — individual and team
        reflections on project achievements, limitations, and improvement
        opportunities.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Critical Reflection Report}
\label{sec:critical_reflection}

\subsection{Methodological Strengths}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Iterative risk loop} — Re-using the same 5×5 matrix
        across Activities 6, 12, 13 created a coherent audit trail and
        simplified stakeholder communication.
  \item \textbf{Cross-department engagement} — Embedding subject-matter
        experts (SCADA, supply chain, R\&D) in each task produced richer
        asset inventories and more realistic recovery metrics.
  \item \textbf{Clause-mapped deliverables} — Aligning every chapter to a
        specific ISO/IEC 27001 clause reduced scope creep and ensured
        audit-readiness of documents.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Challenges Encountered}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{OT/IT convergence complexity} — Mapping Annex A controls
        to mixed OT assets required extensive interpretation and
        sometimes bespoke controls (e.g.\ secure firmware signing).
  \item \textbf{Data granularity versus confidentiality} — Obtaining
        detailed incident data for the KPI dashboard was constrained by
        privacy rules, forcing reliance on aggregated metrics.
  \item \textbf{Resource availability} — Simulated exercises exposed
        single-point-of-knowledge risks (HR‐502) that were underestimated
        in the initial BIA.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Lessons Learned}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Early validation beats late correction} — Pilot tests of
        controls (e.g.\ cloud logging) should run in parallel with policy
        drafting to avoid post-implementation non-conformities.
  \item \textbf{Visual dashboards accelerate executive buy-in} — Simple
        traffic-light KPIs proved more persuasive than detailed
        spreadsheets when presenting to the Board.
  \item \textbf{Continuity plans must remain living documents} — Even
        minor organisational changes (staff churn, new wind farm) rapidly
        outdate contact trees and \ac{RTO}/\ac{RPO} assumptions.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Opportunities for Further Improvement}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Integrate \emph{MITRE ATT\&CK for ICS} to refine threat scenarios
        and better quantify OT-specific residual risks.
  \item Automate evidence collection (backup-verification logs,
        vulnerability-scan results) into the \ac{ISMS} repository to streamline
        future audits.
  \item Extend supplier-security clauses to cover software bill of
        materials (SBOM) for all critical firmware and third-party code.
\end{itemize}
