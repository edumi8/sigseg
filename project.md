### Case Studies - Common Security Information Concerns

#### Current Challenges

##### No formal Information Security Management System

##### Ensuring compliance with applicable EU regulations, namely NIS 2

##### Lack of formal risk assessment and treatment process

##### Limited security awareness among employees

##### No or reduced use of existing security equipment / features already available

##### in the organization

#### Threat Landscape

##### Phishing attacks targeting employees

##### Potential insider threats from employees with access to valuable data

##### Malware and ransomware attacks targeting endpoint devices

##### Data breaches exposing sensitive data

##### AI-driven cybersecurity attacks

### Process Phases

#### The proposed process is structured in followingphases:

#### Phase 1:setting up the ISMS

#### Phase 2:managing the risk

#### Phase 3:preparing for contingencies

#### Phase 4:auditing the ISMS

#### Each phase has severalactivities.

#### Usually activities should bedevelopedin a sequential way, for instance,one in

#### each week.

#### But this organisation is not mandatory, the team should organize the work as best

#### fits their needs.




### Phase 1 - Setting up the ISMS

#### Activity 1:Case Study Selection

#### Activity 2:Scope of the ISMS

#### Activity 3:Information Security Policy and Objectives

#### Activity 4:Other Policies and Procedures




### Activity 1 - Case Study Selection

(^1) Team Formation:students should form groups of 3 students

#### Team Tasks

##### Select (or create) a case study organization

##### Refine / complete the case study regarding

###### Organization governance structure

###### Key departments, its leadership and staff

###### Consider a minimum of 4 departments

###### Each team member is responsible for one department

###### The all team is responsible for the remaining departments

#### Individual Tasks

##### Deep the case study regarding the department you are responsible for

#### Deliverables:

#### Report describing the case study organization, including the team and

#### individual contributions




### Activity 2 - Scope of the ISMS

#### Team Tasks

##### Identify organization stakeholders

##### Describe IT (shared) infrastructure and systems

##### Identify relevant business processes and data

##### Define ISMS boundaries

#### Individual Tasks

##### Describe IT department specific infrastructure and systems

##### Identify department relevant business processes and data

#### Deliverables:

#### Scope of the ISMS (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 4.3 [1])

#### Update the report describing the case study organization




### Activity 3 - Information Security Policy and Objectives

#### Team Tasks

##### Identify stakeholders requirements and business goals

##### Identify security objectives

##### Outline leadership commitment, risk management goals, and compliance

##### requirements

#### Individual Tasks

##### Contribute to the team tasks with specific department insights

#### Deliverables:

#### Information Security Policy (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 4.2 [1])

#### Information Security Objectives (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 6.2 [1])




### Activity 4 - Other Policies and Procedures

#### Team Tasks

##### Elaborate one (organization wide) security policy

##### Elaborate one (organization wide) security procedure

#### Individual Tasks

##### Elaborate one (organization wide) security policy

##### Elaborate one (organization wide) security procedure

#### Deliverables:

#### 4 x Other Security Policy (e.g. ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A 5.10 [1])

#### 4 x Other Security Procedures (e.g. ISO/IEC 27001 - Annex A Clause 5.37

#### [1])




### Phase 2 - Managing the Risk

#### Activity 5:Risk Assessment Framework Setup

#### Activity 6:Assets Inventory

#### Activity 7:Conducting a Risk Assessment

#### Activity 8:Risk Treatment - Statement of Applicability




### Activity 5 - Risk Assessment Framework Setup

#### Team Tasks

##### Elaborate a Risk Assessment Policy

##### Setup a Risk Assessment Process

##### Setup a Risk Treatment Process

#### Individual Tasks

##### Contribute to the team tasks with specific department insights

#### Deliverables:

#### Risk (Assessment) Policy (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 6.1.2 [1])

#### Risk Assessment Process (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 6.1.2 [1])

#### Risk Treatment Process (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 6.1.2 [1])




### Activity 6 - Assets Inventory

#### Team Tasks

##### Create an asset inventory, including systems, software, databases, and sensitive

##### data

##### Assign owners and classify assets based on confidentiality, integrity, and

##### availability

##### (Focus on shared assets)

#### Individual Tasks

##### Contribute to the team tasks with respect to assets specific to the department

##### you are responsible for

#### Deliverables:

#### Inventory of assets (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A 5.9 [1])




### Activity 7 - Conducting a Risk Assessment

#### Team Tasks

##### Identify threats and vulnerabilities on assets

##### Evaluate risks using the defined process (cf. activity 5)

##### (Focus on shared assets)

#### Individual Tasks

##### Identify threats and vulnerabilities on assets

##### Evaluate risks using the defined process (cf. activity 5)

##### (Focus on department specific assets)

#### Deliverables:

#### Risk Assessment report (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 8.2 [1])




### Activity 8 - Risk Treatment - Statement of Applicability

#### Team Tasks

##### Map ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A controls to the identified risks

##### Justify the inclusion or exclusion of controls

##### (Focus on shared assets)

#### Individual Tasks

##### Map ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A controls to the identified risks

##### Justify the inclusion or exclusion of controls

##### (Focus on department specific assets)

#### Deliverables:

#### Statement of Applicability (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 6.1.3 [1])




### Phase 3 - Preparing for Contingencies

#### Activity 9:Conduct a Business Impact Analysis

#### Activity 10:Contingency Planning Policies Development

#### Activity 11:Contingency Plans Development




### Activity 9 - Conduct a Business Impact Analysis

#### Team Tasks

##### Determine the recovery criticality of the business processes

##### Identify the required resources

##### Identify recovery priorities for system resources

##### (Focus on shared assets)

#### Individual Tasks

##### Contribute to the team tasks providing insights regarding the department you

##### are responsible for

#### Deliverables:

#### Business Impact Analysis report (as part of ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A

#### 5.30 [1])




### Activity 10 - Contingency Planning Policies Development

#### Team Tasks

##### Develop a (General) Contingency Planning Policy

#### Individual Tasks

##### Develop a Incident Response Planning Policy or

##### Develop a Disaster Recovery Planning Policy or

##### Develop a Business Continuity Planning Policy

##### (each team member must develop a distinct policy)

#### Deliverables:

#### (General) Contingency Planning Policy

#### Incident Response Planning Policy (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A 5.24

#### [1])

#### Disaster Recovery Planning Policy (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A 5.29

#### [1])

#### Business Continuity Planning Policy (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A 5.30

#### [1])




### Activity 11 - Contingency Plans Development

#### Team Tasks

##### Ensure coherence between all Contingency Policies and Plans

#### Individual Tasks

##### Develop a Incident Response Plan or

##### Develop a Disaster Recovery Plan or

##### Develop a Business Continuity Plan Policy

##### (each team member must develop a distinct plan)

#### Deliverables:

#### Incident Response Planning Plan (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A 5.26 [1])

#### Disaster Recovery Planning Plan (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A 5.29 /

#### 8.14 [1])

#### Business Continuity Planning Plan (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause Annex A 5.30 /

#### 8.14 [1])




### Phase 4 - Auditing the ISMS

#### Activity 12: Conducting a Risk Assessment (with controls)

#### Activity 13: Internal Audit Simulation

#### Activity 14: Management Review Simulation




### Activity 12 - Conducting a Risk Assessment (with controls)

#### Team Tasks

##### Apply some controls to assets (cf. Statement of Applicability)

##### Reevaluate risks using the defined process (cf. activity 5)

##### (Focus on shared assets)

#### Individual Tasks

##### Apply some controls to assets (cf. Statement of Applicability)

##### Reevaluate risks using the defined process (cf. activity 5)

##### (Focus on department specific assets)

#### Deliverables:

#### Report of corrective actions (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 10.2 [1])

#### New Risk Assessment report (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 8.2 [1])




### Activity 13 - Internal Audit Simulation

#### Team Tasks

##### Select some items for auditing and justify

##### Simulate an internal audit

##### (Focus on shared assets)

#### Individual Tasks

##### Select some items for auditing and justify

##### Simulate an internal audit

##### (Focus on department specific items)

#### Deliverables:

#### Internal audit report of corrective actions (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 9.2 [1])




### Activity 14 - Management Review Simulation

#### Team Tasks

##### Simulate an ISMS review by management

##### Recommend further improvements

#### Individual Tasks

##### Contribute to the team tasks

#### Deliverables:

#### Management review report (ISO/IEC 27001 - Clause 9.3 [1])

#### Critical reflection report about the developed work
