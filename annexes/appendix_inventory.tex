\chapter{Asset Inventory}
\label{appendix:asset_inventory}

This appendix provides a consolidated view of GreenGrid Energy's inventory of critical assets, organised by department and mapped to the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A 5.9.

To keep the register concise, we list each asset only once - using the system nomenclature introduced in section \ref{sec:assets}.
Department-specific usage patterns and threat scenarios are then analysed in Chapter \ref{chap:risk_assessment_report}, where the same
asset ID reappears with the vulnerabilities and threats relevant to each business unit. This two-step structure avoids duplication
of effort, while still capturing the different risk profiles that result from how each department interacts with a shared system.
Shared assets include the company-wide \ac{ERP} instance, the central \ac{IT} network stack, and services managed by \ac{HR} and Customer Services.
