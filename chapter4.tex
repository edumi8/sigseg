\chapter{Other Security Policies and Procedures}
\label{chap:other_sec_policies}

In addition to our main Information Security Policy, GreenGrid Energy implements several 
additional organization-wide security policies and corresponding procedures. These measures 
support our compliance with ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A and ensure that all aspects of our security 
posture are addressed consistently across the organization.

\section{Other Security Policies}

\subsection{Access Control Policy}
\textbf{Purpose:} To ensure that access to information assets is granted only to authorized 
individuals in accordance with the principle of least privilege.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Scope:} Applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access GreenGrid 
Energy's information systems.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Policy:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Access rights are assigned based on roles and responsibilities.
    \item All access requests must be formally documented, approved, and periodically reviewed.
    \item \ac{MFA} is required for accessing sensitive systems.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Enforcement:} Failure to comply with access control procedures may result in restricted 
access, mandatory retraining, or disciplinary action, including termination if misuse is severe.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Responsibilities:} The \ac{IT} Department, in collaboration with \ac{HR} and departmental managers, 
is responsible for the implementation and monitoring of access controls.

\subsection{Data Classification Policy}
\textbf{Purpose:} To classify information according to its sensitivity and ensure that it is handled 
appropriately to prevent unauthorized disclosure, modification, or destruction.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Scope:} Applies to all data owned or processed by GreenGrid Energy.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Policy:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Information shall be classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
    \item Handling, storage, and transmission procedures will be defined based on the data classification.
    \item Regular reviews will ensure that classification levels remain accurate and effective.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Enforcement:} Any violation of the data classification guidelines may lead to corrective 
measures such as access restrictions, formal warnings, and potential legal action in case of data breaches.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Responsibilities:} Department heads are responsible for classifying information within their 
areas, while the IT Department ensures secure storage and transmission.

\subsection{Incident Response Policy}
\textbf{Purpose:} To establish a clear process for reporting, managing, and resolving security incidents 
efficiently.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Scope:} All employees and third-party providers who use or manage GreenGrid Energy’s information systems.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Policy:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item All security incidents must be reported immediately to the \ac{IT} Security Team.
    \item An \ac{IRP} is in place to contain, eradicate, and recover from incidents.
    \item A post-incident review is conducted to capture lessons learned and implement corrective measures.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Enforcement:} Non-compliance with incident reporting procedures or failure to follow the response plan 
may result in disciplinary action, including termination, and can lead to further legal consequences if negligence 
results in significant harm.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Responsibilities:} The \ac{IT} Security Team, led by the \ac{CISO}, is responsible for managing incident response 
and ensuring that all incidents are documented and reviewed.

\subsection{Responsible IT Resource Usage Policy}
\textbf{Purpose:} To promote the secure and efficient use of GreenGrid Energy’s IT resources in support 
of business operations while maintaining data integrity.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Scope:} This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff who access or use 
GreenGrid Energy's \ac{IT} systems and devices.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Policy:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \ac{IT} resources should primarily support work-related activities and organizational goals.
    \item Users must protect their access credentials and handle sensitive information responsibly.
    \item Personal use of \ac{IT} resources is allowed only if it does not interfere with work duties or compromise system security.
    \item All \ac{IT} resource usage must comply with applicable laws, regulations, and company policies.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Enforcement:} Violations of this policy may lead to corrective measures such as access suspension, 
formal warnings, mandatory retraining, or disciplinary actions up to and including termination.\\[1mm]
\textbf{Responsibilities:} Department Heads are responsible for ensuring that their teams understand and adhere to 
this policy, while the \ac{IT} Department monitors usage and implements necessary safeguards.

\section{Other Security Procedures}
\label{sec:extra_procedures}

For each of the above policies, the following procedures provide 
detailed, step-by-step instructions for implementation and operational 
management. Beyond simply stating how tasks are carried out, these 
procedures also outline how data produced by or for the \ac{ISMS}—such 
as audit logs, classification labels, or incident records—are securely 
stored, accessed, and disposed of.

\subsection{Access Control Procedure}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Document and Approve Access Requests}: 
    All new access requests must be submitted via the official request 
    form and approved by the department manager and \ac{IT} Security. 
    \item \textbf{Periodic Access Reviews}: 
    Perform quarterly audits to ensure that user privileges align with 
    current roles and responsibilities; remove or adjust access as needed.
    \item \textbf{\ac{MFA}}:
    Enable and maintain \ac{MFA} for sensitive systems, ensuring credentials 
    are stored securely and never shared.
    \item \textbf{Protecting \ac{ISMS} Data}:
    Access logs, approval workflows, and any reports on privileges 
    are labeled \textit{Restricted}, stored in an encrypted repository, 
    and shared only with authorized personnel.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data Classification Procedure}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Identify and Label Assets}: 
    Classify data as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted 
    according to the Data Classification Policy.
    \item \textbf{Storage and Handling Guidelines}:
    For each classification level, follow approved controls 
    (e.g., encryption for Restricted data, restricted folder 
    permissions for Confidential).
    \item \textbf{Periodic Review and Update}:
    Conduct semiannual checks to validate that classification 
    labels remain accurate as business processes evolve.
    \item \textbf{Protecting \ac{ISMS} Data}:
    All classification records and any logs describing data 
    reclassification are stored on a secure server with 
    role-based access. Any reclassification decisions are 
    documented and version-controlled.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Incident Response Procedure}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Immediate Reporting}: 
    All security incidents must be reported to the \ac{IT} Security Team 
    or the Incident Response Coordinator within 15 minutes of discovery.
    \item \textbf{Containment, Eradication, and Recovery}: 
    Follow the \ac{IRP} to isolate affected systems, 
    remove malicious artifacts, and restore operations with minimal 
    downtime.
    \item \textbf{Post-Incident Review}: 
    Conduct a root cause analysis to identify vulnerabilities 
    or process gaps and document lessons learned for continuous 
    improvement.
    \item \textbf{Protecting \ac{ISMS} Data}:
    All incident reports, investigation notes, and evidence are 
    tagged \textit{Confidential}, stored in a secure incident 
    database accessible only to the Incident Response Team, 
    and retained for the legally required duration.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Responsible \ac{IT} Resource Usage Procedure}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Policy Distribution and Acknowledgment}:
    The Responsible \ac{IT} Resource Usage Policy is circulated 
    to all new hires and existing employees; each individual 
    must acknowledge it in writing or via an online form.
    \item \textbf{Usage Monitoring}:
    The \ac{IT} Department periodically reviews network and 
    system logs for suspicious or excessive personal use. 
    Violations are escalated as per HR guidelines.
    \item \textbf{Incident Handling}:
    If a misuse incident is detected, a formal investigation 
    is initiated, and appropriate disciplinary measures 
    (including revoking certain privileges) may be applied.
    \item \textbf{Protecting \ac{ISMS} Data}:
    All logs and records regarding user behavior are labeled 
    at least \textit{Internal} and stored on secure servers. 
    Only HR, the \ac{IT} Security Team, and relevant department 
    managers have access for investigative purposes.
\end{itemize}
