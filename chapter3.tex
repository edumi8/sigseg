\chapter{Information Security Policy and Objectives}
\label{chap:infosec_policy_objectives}
This chapter addresses the next phase of establishing GreenGrid Energy’s
\ac{ISMS} by incorporating
stakeholder requirements, business goals, security objectives, leadership
commitment, and overall compliance needs.

\section{Stakeholder Requirements and Business Goals}
\label{sec:stakeholder_req}

As established in Chapter \ref{chap:isms_scope}, GreenGrid Energy’s stakeholders include
Executive Management, the \ac{IT} Department, Department Heads \& Employees,
Suppliers, Clients, Regulators, and Certifying Bodies. Because the
\ac{ISMS} scope specifically includes:
\begin{itemize}
      \item Internal \ac{IT} and \ac{OT} networks, including \ac{SCADA} and
            enterprise systems used by \textbf{Supply Chain Management},
            \textbf{Customer Services \& Billing}, and \textbf{\ac{RD}} departments.
      \item Data processing within cloud environments generated
            or consumed by \textbf{Supply Chain Management}, \textbf{Customer Services \&
                  Billing}, and \textbf{\ac{RD}}.
      \item Procurement processes and vendor management.
      \item Employee data, access management, and training processes.
\end{itemize}
Most stakeholder requirements focus on ensuring robust controls to protect:
\begin{itemize}
      \item Critical infrastructure.
      \item Sensitive customer and financial data.
      \item Intellectual property from \ac{RD} activities.
      \item Secure procurement of hardware/software through the Supply
            Chain Management department.
      \item Proper handling of employee data and permissions.
\end{itemize}

In contrast, stakeholders agree that \textbf{third-party systems fully managed externally},
\textbf{personal employee devices}, \textbf{physical security at externally managed premises},
and \textbf{separate subsidiaries or partner organizations outside direct oversight} are
explicitly out of scope, thus reducing direct stakeholder interest in those areas.

\section{Leadership Commitment}
\label{sec:leadership_commitment}

GreenGrid Energy’s board of directors reinforce
the \ac{ISMS} by:
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Allocating Sufficient Budgets:} Funding security tools
            and training for departments within the \ac{ISMS} boundaries.
      \item \textbf{Championing Policy Adoption:} Ensuring all employees
            and department heads integrate the new Information Security Policy
            into their daily operations.
      \item \textbf{Encouraging a Security Culture:} Mandating ongoing
            training on secure processes, especially for staff involved in
            vendor management and grid operations.
      \item \textbf{Reviewing \ac{ISMS} Performance:} Conducting periodic
            management reviews to track key risk metrics,
            incident reports, and audit findings.
\end{itemize}

\section{Monitoring and Review of ISMS Performance}
\label{sec:isms_monitoring}

To ensure continuous improvement, GreenGrid Energy will measure and review ISMS effectiveness through:
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Annual ISMS Audit:} Conduct full audits and ensure corrective actions for non-conformities.
      \item \textbf{Key Risk Indicators (KRIs):} Monitor unauthorized access attempts, security incidents, and policy violations.
      \item \textbf{Management Review Meetings:} Perform biannual ISMS performance reviews with Executive Management.
\end{itemize}


\section{Risk Management Goals and Compliance Requirements}
\label{sec:risk_goals_compliance}

Given the potential threats to the included systems and data, the organization
sets forth the following overarching risk management goals:

\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Protect \ac{OT} and \ac{IT} Convergence:} Keep \ac{SCADA}, \ac{IoT}, and
            enterprise networks resilient against cyber threats.
      \item \textbf{Safeguard Data Privacy and Integrity:} Ensure
            confidentiality of customer billing data, vendor records, and
            intellectual property from \ac{RD}.
      \item \textbf{Maintain Regulatory Compliance:} Satisfy relevant
            \ac{EU} directives such as NIS~2, and meet ISO/IEC~27001 standards
            to secure continuous certification and avoid penalties.
      \item \textbf{Mitigate Vendor and Procurement Risks:} Enforce
            security checks during procurement of critical hardware/software,
            and conduct regular vendor risk assessments.
      \item \textbf{Enhance Incident Response Preparedness:} Develop
            rapid containment strategies for security breaches or supply
            chain disruptions, minimizing downtime of grid operations.
\end{itemize}

\section{Information Security Policy (Clause~4.2)}
\label{sec:infosec_policy}

\subsection*{Policy Statement}
GreenGrid Energy is committed to safeguarding the confidentiality,
integrity, and availability of information assets within the boundaries
of its \ac{ISMS}, specifically covering:
\begin{itemize}
      \item Internal \ac{IT} and \ac{OT} networks that power core operations.
      \item Data processed through corporate cloud environments.
      \item Procurement and vendor relationships supporting operational continuity.
      \item Employee access management, training, and \ac{HR}-related records.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Key Principles}
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Risk-Based Controls:} Security measures must be
            tailored to identified threats, focusing on high-risk areas
            such as \ac{SCADA}, third-party supplier links, and \ac{RD} data.
      \item \textbf{Legal and Regulatory Compliance:} Adhere to
            ISO/IEC~27001, \ac{NIS2}, and national data protection laws
            relevant to energy operations.
      \item \textbf{Continuous Improvement:} Policies, controls, and
            training programs will be reviewed at least annually or
            following major incidents.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{\ac{CISO}:} Oversees policy implementation and audits
            across \ac{IT}, \ac{OT}, and supply chain operations.
      \item \textbf{Department Directors:} Ensure departmental processes
            adhere to this policy; sponsor secure practices among staff.
      \item \textbf{All Employees:} Follow approved procedures, maintain
            vigilance for potential threats, and report incidents promptly.
\end{itemize}

\section{Department-Specific Security Objectives (Clause~6.2)}
\label{sec:department_objectives}

Below are the measurable information security objectives for each major
department within the \ac{ISMS} scope. Each subsection also highlights
the primary stakeholders driving or benefiting from these goals.

\subsection{Engineering \& Operations}
\paragraph{Stakeholders:}
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Grid Operations Teams}: Manage real-time grid stability and control systems.
      \item \textbf{SCADA and Industrial Control Engineers}: Maintain secure and reliable SCADA environments.
      \item \textbf{Regulatory Authorities}: Oversee compliance with national and EU grid security regulations.
      \item \textbf{IT Security and Network Teams}: Secure OT/IT convergence to protect grid operations.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Objectives:}
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Objective EO-1: Strengthen SCADA System Security}
            \begin{itemize}
                  \item \textbf{Target:} Ensure 100\% of SCADA systems have up-to-date security patches and network segmentation controls.
                  \item \textbf{Measure:} Compliance rate of SCADA system updates and segmentation audits.
                  \item \textbf{Owner:} SCADA Security Team, \ac{CISO}.
            \end{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Objective EO-2: Improve OT Network Visibility and Anomaly Detection}
            \begin{itemize}
                  \item \textbf{Target:} Deploy real-time monitoring tools to achieve a 90\% anomaly detection rate in OT networks.
                  \item \textbf{Measure:} Number of security alerts detected vs. actual threats mitigated.
                  \item \textbf{Owner:} Engineering Security Lead, IT Security Team.
            \end{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Objective EO-3: Enhance Physical and Cyber Resilience of Grid Infrastructure}
            \begin{itemize}
                  \item \textbf{Target:} Perform biannual security assessments for grid substations and power distribution control centers.
                  \item \textbf{Measure:} Number of identified vulnerabilities mitigated before next assessment cycle.
                  \item \textbf{Owner:} Grid Operations Manager, \ac{CISO}.
            \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}


\subsection{Supply Chain Management}

\paragraph{Stakeholders:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Supply Chain Staff:} Procurement Specialists and Vendor Relationship Managers who oversee supplier performance and risk.
    \item \textbf{Vendors and Third Parties:} Partners required to comply with our stringent security checks, audits, and contractual requirements.
    \item \textbf{Executive Management:} Needs assurance of an uninterrupted, secure supply of components to support operational continuity and protect the company’s reputation.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Objectives:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Objective SC-1: Reduce High-Risk Vendor Gaps} \\
    To lower the risk of compromised components or software infiltrating GreenGrid Energy's infrastructure, it is essential that our critical suppliers adhere to rigorous security standards. This proactive auditing approach helps prevent operational disruptions, regulatory non-compliance, and reputational damage.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Target:} Audit at least 100\% of all critical hardware/software suppliers annually.
        \item \textbf{Measurement:} Track the percentage of audited suppliers, monitor the number and closure time of security findings, and evaluate any security incidents attributable to supplier issues.
        \item \textbf{Owner:} Supply Chain Manager and \ac{CISO}.
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Objective SC-2: Maintain Rapid Supplier Incident Response} \\
    Minimizing supply chain downtime is vital to avoiding customer dissatisfaction and churn. Quick resolution of disruptions ensures that delays do not negatively impact our operations or customer trust.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Target:} Ensure that supply chain incidents are resolved within a maximum of 45 minutes, except in cases of natural disasters or other uncontrollable events.
        \item \textbf{Measurement:} Monitor the average resolution time, count incidents exceeding the 45-minute threshold, and track customer feedback or churn linked to supply chain delays.
        \item \textbf{Owner:} Supply Chain Manager and \ac{CISO}.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Customer Services \& Billing}
\paragraph{Stakeholders:}
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Customer Services Staff}: Manage billing, handle
            customer inquiries and personal data.
      \item \textbf{Clients}: Expect confidentiality of financial and
            personal information.
      \item \textbf{Regulators (e.g., Data Protection Authorities)}:
            Enforce compliance with privacy regulations.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Objectives:}
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Objective CS-1: Enhance Data Privacy Controls}
            \begin{itemize}
                  \item \textbf{Target:} Achieve zero high-severity data breaches
                        in billing systems each quarter.
                  \item \textbf{Measure:} Number of verified privacy incidents
                        rated high severity.
                  \item \textbf{Owner:} Customer Services Director, CISO.
            \end{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Objective CS-2: Improve Customer Incident Handling}
            \begin{itemize}
                  \item \textbf{Target:} Reduce average incident resolution time
                        by 25\% over 12 months for billing-related security issues.
                  \item \textbf{Measure:} Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR) for
                        billing-system incidents.
                  \item \textbf{Owner:} Customer Services Director, \ac{IT} Department.
            \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research \& Development (RD)}
\paragraph{Stakeholders:}
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{\ac{RD} Teams}: Create proprietary analytics
            algorithms and software for grid optimization.
      \item \textbf{Executive Management}: Relies on \ac{RD} innovation
            for market competitiveness.
      \item \textbf{Intellectual Property Owners}: Concerned about
            protecting patents, code repositories, trade secrets.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Objectives:}
\begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Objective RD-1: Prevent Unauthorized Access to
                  \ac{RD} Data}
      It is vital that the innovation data on GreenGrid Enery's Infrastructure remains unaccessed in a non authorized way,
      as such data includes confindential information that could cause reputational damage and eventual software damage on the infrastructure.
            \begin{itemize}
                  \item \textbf{Target:} Achieve a 50\% reduction in unauthorized
                        access attempts and security alerts within the \ac{RD}
                        environment.
                  \item \textbf{Measure:} Number of \ac{RD}-specific security
                        alerts against the prior reporting period.
                  \item \textbf{Owner:} \ac{RD} Manager, \ac{IT} Department.
            \end{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Objective RD-2: Strengthen Security of Environments}
      Since the environments on the \ac{RD} Department are critical, attacks on its infrastructure are to be avoided.
      Strengthening security on the environments ensures the attacks and vulnerabilities don't affect the service provided.
            \begin{itemize}
                  \item \textbf{Target}: Perform quarterly audits of \ac{RD} environments to identify and mitigate vulnerabilities.
                  \item \textbf{Measure}: Audit completion rate.
                  \item \textbf{Owner}: Director of \ac{RD} \ac{IT} Security Team.
            \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
