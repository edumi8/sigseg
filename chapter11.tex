% Activity 12: Conducting a Risk Assessmen
\chapter{Risk Assessment}
\label{chap:activity12}

This chapter documents the risk assessment of the GreenGrid Energy \ac{ISMS}  
It fulfils two ISO/IEC 27001 deliverables:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Corrective-Actions Report} – Clause 10.2  
        (Section~\ref{sec:corrective_actions});
  \item \textbf{New Risk-Assessment Report} – Clause 8.2  
        (Section~\ref{sec:new_risk_assessment}).
\end{enumerate}

The reassessment re-uses the methodology in
Chapter~\ref{chap:risk_management}, applies the Annex A controls already
selected in the Statement of Applicability
(Chapter~\ref{chap:soa}), and evaluates the resulting \emph{residual} risk for:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Shared assets} – enterprise resources used organisation-wide;
  \item \textbf{Department-specific assets} – the \ac{RD}, Supply-Chain and
        Customer Services \& Billing domains.
\end{itemize}

Only changes from the original assessment made on Chapter~\ref{chap:risk_assessment_report}) are shown; unchanged risks are carried forward verbatim.

%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Method Recap}
\label{sec:method_recap}
%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The same 5 × 5 qualitative matrix (Figure~\ref{figure:risk_matrix}) is used.
Residual likelihood and impact are re-scored \emph{after} the control is fully
implemented and evidence (e.g.\ pilot results, audit logs, or training
completion records) is available.

\begin{center}
\emph{Risk appetite remains unchanged: risks rated \textbf{High} or
\textbf{Medium-High} must be further treated, transferred, or avoided.}
\end{center}

\section{Summary and Next Steps}
%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Risk posture improved.}  
        Twelve of the seventeen originally \emph{High} or \emph{Medium-High}
        risks have been reduced to \emph{Medium} or lower.
  \item \textbf{Residual hot-spots.}  
        Four risks remain \emph{Medium-High} (IT-201, RD-102 cloud,
        CS-CRM-01, CS-BILL-02).  They carry dedicated treatment plans and
        deadlines (Table~\ref{tab:corrective_actions}).
  \item \textbf{Continuous monitoring.}  
        All residual risks rated \emph{Medium} or higher are entered into the
        ISMS risk register with quarterly review triggers.
\end{itemize}

The Risk Committee will reconvene in October 2025 (Q4) to verify that the open
corrective actions are closed and to decide whether risk acceptance is now
possible or further treatment is required.
