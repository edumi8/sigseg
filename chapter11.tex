\chapter{Risk Assessment}
\label{chap:risk_assessment}

Following the initial risk assessment presented in Chapter 6 and the Statement of Applicability (SoA) developed in Chapter 7, this activity consists of reassessing the risk landscape after applying selected ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A controls. The objective is to verify risk reduction, validate the effectiveness of the chosen controls, and record any corrective actions required.

\section*{12.1 Methodology}
The methodology defined in Chapter 5 was used for risk re-evaluation, relying on:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The Threat-Vulnerability-Asset (TVA) model for structuring risk identification.
    \item A 5×5 qualitative risk matrix (likelihood × impact) to assess residual risk.
    \item Focused evaluation on shared assets and each team member’s department: Shared Infrastructure, Supply Chain Management, Engineering \& Grid Operations, and Research \& Development.
\end{itemize}

Each risk scenario was reviewed post-control implementation to determine whether:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The residual risk is now acceptable (i.e., low or medium).
    \item Further mitigation or control reinforcement is required.
\end{itemize}

\section*{12.5 Conclusion}
The implementation of Annex A controls significantly reduced risk across most assessed areas. Residual risks were brought to acceptable levels in shared systems and departmental assets, with only a few requiring additional corrective measures. This reassessment confirms the effectiveness of GreenGrid Energy’s ISMS and the adequacy of its control framework.
