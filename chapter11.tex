\chapter{Corrective Action Implementation \& Enterprise-wide Risk Re-assessment}
\label{chap:corrective_and_reassessment}
%--------------------------------------------------------------------
Building on the initial risk analysis in Chapter~\ref{chap:risk_assessment_report} and the Statement of Applicability (\ac{SoA}) prepared in Chapter~\ref{chap:soa}, this chapter re-evaluates the organisation’s threat landscape after the implementation of the selected ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls. The purpose is threefold:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Verify residual-risk reduction}—confirm that each asset’s risk exposure has fallen to an acceptable level; 
  \item \textbf{Validate control effectiveness}—measure how well the chosen controls perform in practice; and
  \item \textbf{Record corrective actions}—document any additional measures needed to close remaining gaps.
\end{itemize}

The reassessment follows the same asset-based methodology detailed in Section~\ref{sec:risk_assessment}. Re-using the original likelihood–impact matrix and scoring criteria ensures direct comparability between the baseline and post-treatment results.
