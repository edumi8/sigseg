\chapter{Risk Assessment}
\label{chap:risk_assessment}

Following the initial risk assessment presented in Chapter \ref{chap:risk_assessment_report} and the \ac{SoA} developed in Chapter \ref{chap:soa}, this chapter consists of reassessing the risk landscape after applying selected ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A controls. The objective is to verify risk reduction, validate the effectiveness of the chosen controls, and record any corrective actions required.

\section{Methodology}
The methodology defined in Chapter \ref{chap:risk_management} was used for risk re-evaluation, relying on:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The \ac{TVA} model for structuring risk identification.
    \item A qualitative matrix, cross-referencing the likelihood of an event with its estimated impact.
    \item Focused evaluation on shared assets and each team member’s department: Shared Infrastructure, Supply Chain Management, Engineering \& Grid Operations, and Research \& Development.
\end{itemize}

Each risk scenario was reviewed post-control implementation to determine whether the residual risk is now considered acceptable or if further mitigation or control reinforcement is required.

\section*{12.5 Conclusion}
The implementation of Annex A controls significantly reduced risk across most assessed areas.
Residual risks were brought to acceptable levels in shared systems and departmental assets, with only a few requiring additional
corrective measures. This reassessment confirms the effectiveness of GreenGrid Energy’s \ac{ISMS} and the adequacy of its control framework.
