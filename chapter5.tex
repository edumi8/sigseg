\chapter{Risk Management Framework}
\label{chap:risk_management}

This chapter outlines the core elements of GreenGrid Energy’s Information Security Risk Management program. It includes the Risk Assessment Policy, the Risk Assessment and Treatment Processes, and a sample Inventory of Assets, developed in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001 Clause 6.1.2 and Annex A 5.9.

\section{Risk Assessment Policy }
\label{sec:risk_policy}

\subsection*{Purpose and Scope}
This policy establishes the approach for identifying, assessing, and treating information security risks at GreenGrid Energy. It applies to all systems, assets, processes, and personnel within the scope of the \ac{ISMS}.

\subsection*{Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Provide a structured and repeatable method for risk identification and analysis.
    \item Support decisions on implementing adequate controls to protect information assets.
    \item Ensure compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Governance and Risk Appetite}
The organization has adopted a conservative risk appetite, especially for critical infrastructure such as \ac{SCADA} systems and customer data. Risks categorized as \textit{High} or \textit{Critical} must be treated with priority and cannot be accepted without mitigation.

\section{Risk Assessment Process}
\label{sec:risk_assessment}

\subsection*{Overview}
The risk assessment process consists of three main steps:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Risk Identification}: Mapping assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and potential impacts.
    \item \textbf{Risk Analysis}: Evaluating likelihood and consequence of threats exploiting vulnerabilities.
    \item \textbf{Risk Evaluation}: Comparing identified risk levels against the organization's risk acceptance criteria.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Methodology}
Risks are analyzed using a \textbf{Risk Level Matrix} that combines the likelihood of an event with the severity of its consequence. The matrix uses a 5x5 scale, where risk levels are categorized as Low, Medium, High, or Critical. This allows for a visual and systematic assessment.

\section{Risk Treatment Process}
\label{sec:risk_treatment}

\subsection*{Treatment Options}
Identified risks that exceed acceptable levels must be treated using one or more of the following strategies:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mitigation}: Reducing risk through controls and safeguards.
    \item \textbf{Acceptance}: Acknowledging low risks with minimal impact.
    \item \textbf{Transference}: Outsourcing or insuring against the risk.
    \item \textbf{Avoidance}: Eliminating the risk by discontinuing risky activities.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Implementation}
For each risk requiring treatment, a formal plan is created defining:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The selected treatment strategy.
    \item Responsible stakeholders.
    \item Implementation timeline and monitoring procedures.
\end{itemize}


\section{Inventory of Assets}
\label{sec:inventory}

A structured inventory of information assets is essential to support the organization’s risk management and treatment activities. This section provides a description of four key assets considered relevant to the scope of the \ac{ISMS} at GreenGrid Energy. Each asset is described according to its classification, use, ownership, governance responsibilities, and associated security concerns.

\subsection*{\ac{SCADA} GridLink System}
The \ac{SCADA} GridLink system is a combination of embedded hardware and software used by the Engineering \& Grid Operations department to perform real-time monitoring and control of the power grid infrastructure. It is classified as \textbf{Critical}, given its direct impact on grid stability and energy distribution. A compromise of this system could lead to widespread outages or unauthorized manipulation of power flows. Security measures include strict OT/IT network segmentation, physical access control, secure remote access protocols, and frequent firmware patching.

\textbf{Governance:} The Engineering \& Grid Operations department is responsible for the asset’s lifecycle, supported by the \ac{CISO} and Security Operations team. Governance ensures alignment with national infrastructure protection regulations and internal operational continuity policies.

\textbf{Compliance:} Must comply with NIS2 Directive (EU) and ISO/IEC 27019 for energy sector control systems.

\subsection*{Microsoft 365 Suite}
Microsoft 365 is a cloud-based productivity suite used by all departments across GreenGrid Energy for communication, document editing, cloud storage, and collaborative workflows. The asset is categorized as \textbf{Important}, due to its high usage and potential to expose internal documents and communications. The IT Department is responsible for its administration. Key risks include credential compromise through phishing attacks, unauthorized file sharing, and improper access control. The system is protected using Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), conditional access policies, and centralized logging.

\textbf{Governance:} Managed by the IT Department in coordination with the \ac{ISMS} team, ensuring secure configurations and incident response readiness.

\textbf{Compliance:} Must adhere to ISO/IEC 27001, 27017 (for cloud security), and internal Acceptable Use and Identity Management policies.

\subsection*{Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Database}
The CRM Database, managed by the Customer Services \& Billing department, stores personally identifiable information (PII), billing history, and service contracts. This asset is classified as \textbf{Highly Confidential}, as it involves sensitive customer data regulated by data protection laws such as the GDPR. Security measures include encryption at rest and in transit, strict role-based access control, regular auditing of access logs, and automated data retention enforcement.

\textbf{Governance:} Ownership lies with the Customer Services \& Billing department, under oversight from the Data Protection Officer (DPO) and \ac{CISO} for regulatory compliance and security posture.

\textbf{Compliance:} Must fully comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), ISO/IEC 27701, and applicable data retention and breach notification laws.

\subsection*{SAP ERP System}
The SAP ERP system is a business-critical software platform used primarily by the Supply Chain Management department for handling procurement, inventory control, invoicing, and vendor management. This asset is classified as \textbf{Important}, as it directly supports financial and operational continuity. The system processes sensitive financial data and operational records, which must be protected from unauthorized access, internal misuse, or misconfiguration.

\textbf{Governance:} Managed by the Supply Chain team in collaboration with the Enterprise Applications Unit within IT. The internal Audit and Compliance Office is responsible for ensuring traceability and segregation of duties.

\textbf{Compliance:} Subject to ISO/IEC 27001, and internal financial control requirements.

\subsection*{Classification and Prioritization Approach}
All assets within the \ac{ISMS} are evaluated using a classification model based on the Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) triad. The classification levels used include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Critical} – Systems that must remain operational at all times to maintain business continuity.
    \item \textbf{Highly Confidential} – Assets containing sensitive or regulated information.
    \item \textbf{Important} – Supporting systems and tools that have a moderate to high impact if compromised.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Inventory Governance}
Each asset has a designated asset owner and a responsible governance entity. The \ac{ISMS} team coordinates with these asset owners to ensure that classification, protection, and lifecycle management are documented and reviewed periodically.

\textbf{Governance Responsibilities:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{\ac{SCADA} GridLink:} Engineering \& Grid Operations, with oversight from \ac{CISO} and Regulatory Affairs.
    \item \textbf{Microsoft 365:} IT Department and \ac{ISMS} Team.
    \item \textbf{CRM Database:} Customer Services \& Billing, DPO, and \ac{CISO}.
    \item \textbf{SAP ERP System:} Supply Chain Management, Internal Audit, and IT Enterprise Systems.
\end{itemize}

The asset inventory is maintained in a centralized repository and reviewed quarterly, or whenever significant changes occur in infrastructure, ownership, or regulatory obligations.
