\chapter{Internal Audit Simulation}
\label{chap:internal_audit_simulation}

As part of Phase 4 of the \ac{ISMS} lifecycle, this activity simulates an internal audit to evaluate the effectiveness, adequacy, and compliance of GreenGrid Energy’s ISMS against ISO/IEC 27001 requirements. The audit also assesses the integration of security controls applied in previous phases and the organisation’s readiness for external certification.

\section{Objectives and Scope}
The internal audit aimed to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Verify conformity with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements, including internal policies and procedures.
    \item Evaluate the effectiveness of implemented Annex A controls.
    \item Confirm that identified risks are being properly monitored, treated, or mitigated.
    \item Assess preparedness for external audit and certification.
\end{itemize}

The scope of this audit includes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Shared IT infrastructure and assets.
    \item Department-specific processes from Engineering \& Grid Operations, R\&D, and Supply Chain Management.
    \item Security controls evaluated in the updated risk assessment (Activity 12).
\end{itemize}

\section{Audit Methodology}
The internal audit followed these steps:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Preparation}: Review of policies, SoA, risk register, and previous assessment reports.
    \item \textbf{Interviews}: Conducted with asset owners, department representatives, and IT staff.
    \item \textbf{Document Review}: Audit of logs, procedures, security events, and access records.
    \item \textbf{Control Testing}: Sample testing of security control enforcement (e.g., MFA, backups, segmentation).
    \item \textbf{Reporting}: Documentation of findings, non-conformities, observations, and recommendations.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Key Findings}
\subsection{Positive Observations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Controls related to access management (Annex A 5.15) and encryption (A.8.4) were effectively implemented across shared systems.
    \item Supply Chain Management demonstrated excellent control over vendor audits and contract compliance.
    \item SCADA systems are protected by network segmentation and monitoring tools, consistent with defined objectives.
    \item R\&D environments are isolated and use code repositories with strict version control and access policies.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Non-Conformities}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{VPN Certificate Lifecycle}: No documented schedule for certificate renewal.
    \item \textbf{SCADA Segmentation Policy}: Some firewall rules were not fully aligned with defined segmentation strategy.
    \item \textbf{Audit Logs in R\&D}: Logging exists but lacks real-time analysis and alerts.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Observations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item There is room for improvement in cross-departmental communication regarding incident response escalation.
    \item Physical access control to server rooms (although partially out of scope) should be reinforced.
\end{itemize}

\section{Recommendations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Establish a formal renewal policy for VPN certificates with automated alerts.
    \item Review and update SCADA segmentation rules and conduct periodic firewall audits.
    \item Integrate SIEM capabilities with R\&D environments to enable real-time monitoring.
    \item Update the Incident Response Plan to clarify responsibilities across departments.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
The internal audit confirmed that the ISMS implemented at GreenGrid Energy is aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 requirements and is effective in mitigating critical risks. Identified non-conformities are limited in scope and can be resolved within a short timeframe. The organisation demonstrates a high degree of maturity in key areas such as supply chain security, SCADA protection, and information classification, indicating a strong position ahead of formal external certification.
