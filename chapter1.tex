\chapter{GreenGrid Energy: Organizational Overview}
\section{Introduction}

GreenGrid Energy is a large-scale enterprise \cite{Portugal2020} specialized in renewable energy, with particular expertise in smart grid technology. The company employs approximately 400 professionals and has achieved revenues of around 35 million euros in the latest fiscal year. Its business activities are structured around three core revenue streams:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Smart grid infrastructure deployment (45\%),
    \item Renewable energy supply contracts (40\%),
    \item Energy monitoring and analytics software (15\%).
\end{enumerate}

With the expanding adoption of renewable energy solutions and increasing integration of \ac{IoT} devices, GreenGrid Energy operates at the intersection of \ac{OT} and \ac{IT}. This convergence necessitates a comprehensive cybersecurity approach, robust governance frameworks, and specialized departmental oversight.

To effectively respond to these challenges, GreenGrid Energy has assembled a dedicated project team to assess and strengthen information security practices within critical departments, while collaboratively addressing overall cybersecurity objectives.

\section{Governance and Leadership}
GreenGrid Energy follows a governance model that balances strategic oversight with efficient operations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Board of Directors}: Define strategic vision, approves major investments, and ensures compliance with energy sector regulations.
    \item \textbf{\ac{CEO}}: Oversees day-to-day operations and reports directly to the Board.
    \item \textbf{Executive Management Team}:
          \begin{itemize}
              \item \emph{\ac{CFO}}: Handles budget planning, financial compliance, and resource allocation.
              \item \emph{\ac{CTO}}: Determines technological direction, including research initiatives and product development.
              \item \emph{\ac{COO}}: Manages core operations, covering engineering, supply chain, and service delivery.
              \item \emph{\ac{CIO}}: Aligns IT strategy with business objectives and oversees IT infrastructure.
              \item \emph{\ac{CISO}}: Develops cybersecurity strategies and enforces best practices across \ac{OT} and \ac{IT} domains.
          \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Department Directors or Managers}: Lead their respective divisions and report to the relevant C-level executive.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Departments}
GreenGrid Energy employs a total of 400 staff members. Of these, 12 serve in leadership roles across the Board of Directors and C-level positions, leaving 388 employees distributed among the following six core departments. Each department is led by a Director or Manager, who reports to the relevant executive.

\subsection{Engineering \& Grid Operations}

The Engineering \& Grid Operations department at GreenGrid Energy is responsible for designing, maintaining, and optimizing the physical power distribution network. This includes real-time monitoring of energy flow, grid stability, and operational efficiency. The department plays a critical role in integrating renewable energy sources into the smart grid infrastructure while ensuring compliance with regulatory standards.

\subsubsection{Core Responsibilities}

The department's key responsibilities include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Grid Infrastructure Management}: Overseeing the deployment, maintenance, and upgrades of smart grid components, including substations, transmission lines, and \ac{DER}.
    \item \textbf{\ac{SCADA} System Operations}: Ensuring continuous monitoring and control of grid assets using \ac{SCADA} systems to maintain grid stability and prevent outages.
    \item \textbf{Grid Security and Reliability}: Implementing resilience measures to protect against cybersecurity threats, natural disasters, and system failures.
    \item \textbf{\ac{OT} Security}: Managing the convergence of \ac{IT} and \ac{OT} environments by deploying security controls for grid-connected devices and industrial control systems.
    \item \textbf{Incident Response and Disaster Recovery}: Developing contingency plans to mitigate risks associated with grid failures, cyberattacks, and supply chain disruptions.
    \item \textbf{Integration of Renewable Energy}: Managing the technical challenges of incorporating solar, wind, and other renewable energy sources into the smart grid.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Infrastructure and Technology Stack}

The Engineering \& Grid Operations department relies on the following core technologies:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{\ac{SCADA} Systems}: Used for real-time monitoring and control of critical grid components.
    \item \textbf{\ac{IoT} Sensors and Smart Meters}: Providing detailed performance data from field assets to optimize energy distribution.
    \item \textbf{Edge Computing}: Enabling localized data processing for low-latency decision-making.
    \item \textbf{Hybrid Cloud Platforms (AWS/Azure)}: Supporting predictive analytics, system automation, and secure data storage.
    \item \textbf{Firewall and \ac{IDS}/\ac{IPS} Solutions}: Ensuring network security through robust threat detection mechanisms.
    \item \textbf{\ac{OT}/\ac{IT} Network Segmentation}: Implementing strict security controls to prevent unauthorized access between corporate \ac{IT} and operational networks.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Departmental Management Structure}

The Engineering \& Grid Operations department is led by an \textbf{Engineering \& Grid Operations Manager}, who reports directly to the \ac{COO}. The internal structure is organized as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Engineering \& Grid Operations Manager}: Provides strategic oversight, ensures grid stability, and aligns department objectives with company goals.
    \item \textbf{Grid Infrastructure Lead}: Oversees grid modernization projects, substation maintenance, and infrastructure expansion efforts.
    \item \textbf{SCADA and Control Systems Specialist}: Manages SCADA operations, ensuring continuous monitoring, control, and security of grid systems.
    \item \textbf{OT Security Engineer}: Focuses on securing grid-connected assets and mitigating cyber threats targeting operational technology.
    \item \textbf{Renewable Energy Integration Specialist}: Ensures seamless incorporation of renewable energy sources, optimizing grid balancing and energy distribution.
    \item \textbf{Field Operations Supervisor}: Coordinates on-site maintenance teams, ensuring safety compliance and operational efficiency in power distribution.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research \& Development Department}
Focused on innovation and next-generation solutions, the \ac{RD} department is dedicated to advancing smart grid technology, predictive analytics, and renewable energy systems through proprietary innovations.
It ensures secure environments for technology development and rigorously safeguards intellectual property against cybersecurity threats.

\subsubsection{Organizational Structure}

To address the responsibilities of the \ac{RD} department, its management includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Director of \ac{RD}}: Reports directly the developments to the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) and ensures that all research initiatives align with the overall strategic objectives.
    \item \textbf{Algorithm Development Specialists}: Design, develop, and optimize algorithms for smart grid optimization, predictive analytics, and renewable energy management.
    \item \textbf{Cybersecurity and Intellectual Property Management Specialists}: Safeguard proprietary research outputs, perform security assessments, and ensure proper protection of intellectual property.
    \item \textbf{Prototype Testing Specialists}: Conduct rigorous testing of prototypes in controlled environments to validate functionality, performance, and compliance with security standards.
\end{itemize}
Besides the organizational structure presented, there is a strong cross-department collaboration with Engineering \& Grid Operations department, to facilitate the transition of validated technologies from the development stage to practical, operational environments.

\subsection{Customer Services \& Billing}
All client-facing activities—such as account management, billing, and technical support—fall under this department's purview, serving both residential and enterprise customers. Protecting personal and financial data is paramount, requiring rigorous compliance with data privacy regulations and prompt issue resolution to maintain customer satisfaction.

\subsection{Supply Chain Management Department}

The Supply Chain Management department at GreenGrid Energy plays a crucial role in supporting the company's operational efficiency and strategic goals. Its primary responsibilities include procuring, assessing, and managing the acquisition of hardware, software, and external services necessary for the company's operations. Given the company's strong emphasis on cybersecurity and compliance, this department ensures that all acquired resources and services adhere to rigorous security standards and regulatory requirements.

To address the complexity of procurement and vendor management activities effectively, the Supply Chain Management department is organized with clearly defined roles under the supervision of a Supply Chain Manager, who directly reports to the \ac{COO}. The internal structure of this department is as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Supply Chain Manager}: Responsible for strategic oversight, departmental leadership, and alignment of procurement processes with organizational objectives. Coordinates overall procurement strategies and supervises team performance.

    \item \textbf{Hardware Procurement Specialist}: Oversees the procurement, logistics, inventory management, and verification of all physical hardware components, such as smart-grid devices, sensors, and power management equipment. Ensures hardware security integrity and vendor reliability.

    \item \textbf{Software Procurement Specialist}: Manages the procurement of software assets, licenses, analytics tools, and cybersecurity solutions. Collaborates closely with the \ac{CIO} and \ac{CTO} to confirm software solutions align with technology strategies and cybersecurity guidelines.

    \item \textbf{Service Procurement Specialist}: Focuses on acquiring external services critical to operations, such as consulting engagements, technical training programs, support contracts, and maintenance services. Responsible for monitoring the quality and compliance of service providers.

    \item \textbf{Vendor Relationship Specialist}: Dedicated to managing strategic vendor relationships across hardware, software, and services. Conducts vendor risk assessments, security audits, and compliance verifications to mitigate supply chain-related risks.
\end{itemize}

This structure ensures clear accountability, specialization of roles, and scalability to meet future operational demands. It also aligns closely with GreenGrid Energy's cybersecurity framework by ensuring thorough vetting and continuous monitoring of all vendors and acquired resources.

HR oversees recruitment, training, performance evaluation, and overall employee welfare. This department ensures that GreenGrid Energy remains compliant with evolving industry regulations and standards. \section{Infrastructure and Security Context}

\subsection{Core Technologies and Systems}

GreenGrid Energy's infrastructure comprises several critical technologies supporting operational excellence, security, and departmental integration.

\paragraph{\ac{SCADA} Systems}
These systems allow real-time control and monitoring of grid equipment, essential for the reliability and stability of energy distribution operations.

\paragraph{Hybrid Cloud Platforms (AWS/Azure)}
Offer scalable computing, secure data storage, advanced analytics, and support for \ac{IoT} device management, facilitating innovation and departmental collaboration.

\paragraph{Edge Computing}
Provides local data processing capabilities near physical assets, ensuring low latency and rapid response for critical grid management tasks.

\paragraph{Microsoft 365}
The primary platform for internal communication, collaboration, and productivity across all company departments.

\paragraph{\ac{SAP} \ac{ERP}}
Integrates business-critical functions such as procurement, inventory management, customer services, financial oversight, and regulatory compliance.

\subsection{Network Topology}

GreenGrid Energy maintains two distinct networks to segregate operational and corporate functions securely:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{\ac{OT} Network}: Hosts grid-specific operations, including \ac{SCADA} systems and field-deployed \ac{IoT} devices.
    \item \textbf{\ac{IT} Network}: Manages corporate services such as \ac{ERP} systems, collaboration tools, and \ac{HR} applications.
\end{itemize}

Secure segmentation between the \ac{OT} and \ac{IT} networks is essential to safeguard critical infrastructure operations.

\subsection{Threat Landscape}

The primary cybersecurity threats facing GreenGrid Energy include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Nation-State Actors}: Target critical infrastructure for strategic objectives and disruption.
    \item \textbf{\ac{APTs}}: Sophisticated attackers leveraging vulnerabilities in IoT and grid-related technologies.
    \item \textbf{Supply Chain Risks}: Compromise of hardware or software components through third-party suppliers.
\end{itemize}
